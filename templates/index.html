SIMPLE 4-STEP PROCESS

How It Works

Fast, transparent, and hassle-free government crop procurement

1. Register & Select Crop

Register with your Farmer UID, select crop type (Wheat, Paddy, Oilseeds), and enter estimated yield in Quintals.

2. Book Slot & Get Token

Choose your nearest Mandi/PACS center, select convenient date and time window, and instantly generate digital Token (e.g. A104).

3. Smart Queue Tracking

Check live queue status and predicted waiting time from home. Arrive right when your turn is approaching.

4. Weighing & Direct DBT

Instant weighbridge & quality verification at the center, followed by guaranteed MSP payment credit directly to your bank.
